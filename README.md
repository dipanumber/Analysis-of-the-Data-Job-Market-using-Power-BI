# Analysis-of-the-Data-Job-Market-using-Power-BI
## Project Overview
The growing demand for data-related roles has made it increasingly important for job seekers to understand current job market trends. This project uses Microsoft Power BI to analyze data job market information and present key insights through KPIs, and interactive dashboards.
The analysis focuses on job demand, salary trends, experience levels, hiring locations, and in-demand skills to help users make more informed, data-driven career decisions.
## 1. Problem Statement
Job seekers and professionals transitioning into data-related careers often face challenges due to fragmented job market information. Salary levels, job demand, required skills, and hiring locations can vary significantly across roles, making it difficult to quickly understand the overall market. This project aims to provide a centralized and interactive view of the data job market.
## 2. Objectives
•	Analyze job market trends for data-related roles.

•	Identify high-demand and high-paying job positions.

•	Analyze salary trends across roles, locations, and experience levels.

•	Identify the most in-demand skills.

•	Develop key performance indicators (KPIs) for quick insights.

•	Enable data-driven career and job-search decisions.
## 3. Target Users
•	Job seekers.

•	Job transitioners.

•	Career planners and hiring professionals.
## 4. Dataset
The dataset contains information related to data job postings, including:

•	Job Title.

•	Location.

•	Salary.

•	Experience Level.

•	Required Skills.
## 5. Methodology
The project was completed using the following workflow:
Data Import & Cleaning – Imported and cleaned the dataset using Power Query, including handling missing values, formatting data, and preparing fields for analysis.
   
Data Modeling – Created relationships and structured the dataset for effective analysis.

DAX Analysis – Developed calculated measures and KPIs using DAX.

Dashboard Development – Designed an interactive Power BI dashboard to visualize job market trends and insights.

Insight Generation – Analyzed the results to identify trends in job demand, salaries, locations, experience levels, and skills.
## 6. Key Performance Indicators
The dashboard includes the following KPIs:
Total Job Openings – Total number of data-related jobs
Average Salary – Mean salary across all data roles
Highest Paying Role – Role with the highest average salary
Top Hiring Location – Location with the most job postings
Most In-Demand Skill – Skill appearing most frequently in job postings
## 7. Dashboard Overview
The dashboard includes:
•	KPI cards for quick insights.

•	Job distribution by role.

•	Salary comparison by role and location.

•	Experience-level analysis.

•	Skill demand analysis.

•	Interactive slicers.
## 8. Key Insights
•	Data Analyst and Data Scientist roles dominate the market.

•	Senior-level positions offer higher compensation.

•	SQL and Python are the most in-demand skills.

•	Certain locations show higher job concentration.
## 9.Conclusion
This project demonstrates the effective use of Power BI KPIs and dashboards to analyze job market trends and support informed career decisions.
